# Hanafi dua catalog — source and completeness contract

The new catalog is separate from legacy lessons, at `app/src/main/assets/duas/duas-v1.json`. It contains seven categories, 24 subcategories and 66 unique topics. Forty-six Arabic payloads were compared with directly fetched hadith/Quran pages; three additional owner-supplied payloads are preserved exactly. Seventeen topics intentionally have no Arabic text: source/grade/formula verification is incomplete. These are review entries, not completed recitations. No audio recordings or pronunciation lines have been invented.

`source-checks.json` records URLs, access date and Arabic hashes. `owner-texts.json` is an exact-text fixture. Source verification means an online comparison, **not scholarly approval or verification against a printed edition**. Hadith grades name the collection or grader actually consulted. Quranic passages use `QURAN`, never a fabricated hadith grade. Modern English site translations are not redistributed.

New meaning translations are drafts, explicitly marked for language review. Missing translation/pronunciation fields stay empty. The UI must display a localized missing/review notice rather than silently substituting Persian. Operational titles may fall back to English when a title translation is absent; this must be disclosed. None of the new translations is represented as scholar/native approved.

The first supplied text has verified Tirmidhi 3502 provenance and Tirmidhi's hasan-gh arib classification, but its supplied spelling differs in several places from the online edition. Its text-review flag remains. The second retains the required grade-review flag and the unnumbered source claim. The third matches Muslim 483 online; its requested source-review flag remains pending printed comparison. None enters reminders while the blocking flag remains.

Verified repeated recitations include istighfar three times (Muslim 591), the requested 33/33/34 pattern (Muslim 596a), and daily tasbih one hundred times (Bukhari 6405). A count is not assigned from memory. Witr stays separate from the ordinary-prayer guide; there is no regular Fajr qunut. Friday's sevenfold short-surah item has no recitation or recommendation pending isnad review. No invented special dhikr is assigned to other weekdays.

Validation at this commit: build, **204/204 unit tests**, lint **65 warnings / 0 errors**, Android-test compilation and complete catalog/source/font/content checks passed. Seven new unit tests cover Arabic/Persian normalization, Bengali preservation, source/grade gates, honest language fallback, weekday selection, counters and recent-history bounds. Runtime Android parsing, navigation and visual behavior still require the upcoming reader integration and device checks.
